Night Guards in NYC

Update

Night guards in NYC are custom oral appliances that cushion the forces of grinding and clenching to help protect teeth and dental restorations. At Cosmetic Dentistry NYC - Dr. Pia Lieb, guards are made from impressions of your teeth and fitted to your bite.

A protective night guard and a TMJ splint serve different purposes. A night guard does not stop grinding itself; a TMJ appliance is designed around jaw support and function. The type and material depend on the clinical findings, and a poorly fitting appliance can worsen symptoms.

Explore custom night guards and TMJ guards at the Manhattan practice, then arrange an examination to discuss which appliance may fit your teeth and jaw needs.
